ABOUT US

Our about us

About my shop, Anime Haven. We bring you the finest selection of ways to engage in anime and manga fandoms, discussions and more.
My name is Sofia Martinez, and I am a lifelong Anime fan and Manga reader.
I wanted to create something that would reflect my passions but also let me share in them
with others and give back to the community.
I also wanted to make a wholely accessible website, as I myself have autism.
So here we are today! Running the world's best anime shop (not just in my opinions),
and providing a service us anime fans so sorely needed.
I hope you love it as much as I do!
